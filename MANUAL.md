# Starfall Meadow — Instruction Booklet

November 10, 2026

## Welcome

On clear nights, shooting stars drop star-seeds into the meadow. Inside each one sleeps a little critter. Raise it, play with it and look after it, and when its time is over it rises into the sky as a new star. How well you care for it decides what it grows into, and how brightly its star shines.

This booklet covers everything in the game. The secret forms, rare moonflowers and rare fireflies are only hinted at in the main pages; if you get stuck, the answers are in **Riddle answers** at the very back.

## Getting started

- **Title screen:** CONTINUE plays your saved critter. NEW SEED starts over with a new star-seed (it asks twice, and your journal is kept). JOURNAL opens the journal.
- **Hatching:** keep the star-seed company and it hatches in about half a minute. Then give it a name of up to 6 letters. SELECT suggests a name, START accepts it.
- **Time:** a day in the game lasts about 16½ minutes: 12 of daytime and 4½ of night. With SPEED: FAST in the START menu, everything runs 2.5 times as fast and a day lasts about 6½ minutes. Time passes in the room and the yard, and stands still in menus, the shop, the bag, the journal, stories and minigames. It only passes while the game is switched on.
- **Age:** your critter ages one year for every day in the game. The status card shows its age, for example "AGE: 1 YEAR" or "AGE: 5 YEARS".
- **Saving:** the game saves by itself as you play. To stop, press START and choose SAVE AND QUIT before switching off.

## The icons

The eight icons sit along the top and bottom of the screen. Move between them with the +Control Pad and press A.

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| FEED | Give a MEAL to fill hunger, or a SNACK to cheer it up. Too many snacks make it overweight and can make it sick. |
| LIGHTS | Turn the lights off when it falls asleep, and back on in the morning. |
| PLAY | Pick one of four minigames and win coins (see **Minigames**). |
| MEDS | Cure it when it is sick. Sometimes it needs a second dose. Medicine when it isn't sick only makes it pull a yuck face. |
| CLEAN | Wash away the mess. A messy room soon makes it sick. |

| PET | Answer it when it calls you. |
| ! | Lights up when it needs something, and it cries. Answer it soon. |

## The status card

STATUS shows how your critter is doing:

- **Hunger and happiness:** up to four hearts each. Keep both from running empty.
- **Bond:** how close it feels to you, up to four hearts. Petting it when it calls, gifts, bedtime stories and great minigame rounds all raise it.
- **Age and weight:** its age in years, and its weight. An overweight critter is not well cared for.
- **The care corner** (bottom right):
  - The **heart line** shows what it gets most of: OUTSIDE, PLANTS, PLAY, or BALANCED when it's a bit of everything. This decides what a well-cared-for critter grows into.
  - The **! line** appears when one need keeps running out lately: HUNGRY, UNHAPPY, MESSY, SICK or LIGHTS ON. Fix that one first.

**Health.** Its health is hidden: there is no meter for it. It only drops while your critter is sick, or while its hunger is empty, for more than a few moments. When it falls below about a third, your critter tells you ("KIWI LOOKS WEAK... IT NEEDS CARE!"): cure it and feed it right away, because if it reaches zero, your critter runs away. The rest of the time it heals slowly by itself, and faster with rest decor or a good round of Stargazer.

## Good care

Your critter's needs are checked every 15 seconds or so. Each check counts as good care when it is fed, happy, clean, healthy, not overfed, and asleep with the lights off at night. The share of good checks is its **care score**, which decides its teen form and how brightly its star shines at the end.

- **Health is hidden.** It drops only while it is sick or its hunger is empty, so cure it and feed it quickly (see The status card).
- **Sickness** comes from a messy room and too many snacks. Use MEDS, or a VITAMIN from the bag, which also keeps it from getting sick for the rest of the day.
- **Weight** goes up with sweet food and comes down with SALAD and Hop Hop.

Everything you buy, and every minigame, helps one of five kinds of care. The shop and bag show which.

| Kind | What it covers |
|---|---|
| FOOD | Meals and snacks. Food decor gives +1 hunger when it's used. |
| PLAY | Games, toys and play decor. It counts toward growing into a Fluffbud. |
| CLEAN | Baths and a fresh room. Clean decor makes a mess slower to make it sick. |
| REST | Comfort and quiet. Rest decor heals it a little. |
| OUTSIDE | Time in the yard and around plants. It counts toward an Oakhart or a Dunerose. |
| DECOR | Just to look at (the portrait also gives bond). |

## Growing up

Your critter grows while it is awake.

| Stage | Form | About how long |
|---|---|---|
| Egg | The star-seed | Half a minute |
| Baby | **Bliplet** | Half a day |
| Child | **Sproutle** | A day |
| Teen | **Bloomle** or **Thornle** | Two days |
| Grown-up | One of seven forms | About 12 years |

- **The teen:** a Sproutle cared for well (a care score of 70% or more) becomes a **Bloomle**. One that had a harder time becomes a **Thornle**.
- **The grown-up:** a Bloomle grows into whatever it got most of, and a Thornle into whatever it missed most:

| Teen | Got the most of / missed the most | Grown-up |
|---|---|---|
| Bloomle | Time outside | **Oakhart** |
| Bloomle | Plants around it | **Dunerose** |
| Bloomle | Games and play | **Fluffbud** |
| Bloomle | A bit of everything | **Frillo** |
| Thornle | It was sad a lot | **Thornhog** |
| Thornle | It lived in a mess | **Smogwool** |
| Thornle | It went hungry | **Gunkbill** |

- **Secret forms:** every grown-up has a secret form it can turn into if it gets one special kind of care as a grown-up. One grown-up kept almost perfectly may become something rarer still. Before you find one, its page in the journal's GUIDE shows a riddle; after, it shows the exact rule.
- **Boy or girl:** every critter is a boy or a girl, which changes a small detail of how it looks and the pitch of its cry.

## Day and night

Each day ends with a night that lasts about 4½ minutes (about 1 minute 50 seconds on FAST). At night the clock moves more slowly, but your critter's needs keep their usual pace. When your critter gets sleepy, turn the lights off; turn them on again in the morning. While it sleeps there is still plenty to do:

- **A wishing star** crosses the sky on some nights.
- **A bedtime story** can be read from the SELECT menu, one a night.
- **The moonflower garden** can be tended from the SELECT menu while it sleeps.
- **Fireflies** can be caught in the yard, up to three rounds a night.
- **The moon** changes shape over eight nights, from full to new and back. Watch the window or the yard's sky.

## Wishing stars

About one night in three, a single shooting star slides across the window (or the yard's sky) at some point in the night, with a soft twinkling sound and the words **A SHOOTING STAR! A: MAKE A WISH**. Press **A** while it crosses, or for about 3 seconds after, to make a wish: **+5 coins**. If you're in a menu or a firefly round when it's due, it waits until you're back. The journal counts every wish you make, and a wish has two more uses: one in the garden and one with the fireflies.

## Bedtime stories

At night, choose **STORY** from the SELECT menu (from the baby stage on). There are three stories of the meadow, each six pages long:

- **The First Star-Seed:** why loved critters become stars.
- **Why the Moon Changes:** the moon, its phases and the moonflowers.
- **The Faint Stars:** sad goodbyes, and where wishing stars come from.

A or RIGHT turns the page, LEFT goes back, and B closes the book. Stories you have read get a small star in the list. **There is one story a night**, and it gives **+1 bond** when your critter wakes up. After that, STORY is greyed out until the next night.

## The moonflower garden

While your critter is asleep at night, the SELECT menu's first choice becomes **GARDEN**. It takes you out to the yard, where three soil patches sit at different spots in the grass, behind any yard decor. At first you're just looking. Press **A** to tend the moonflowers: a cursor appears, LEFT and RIGHT pick a patch, and A plants, waters or takes a closer look. **B** puts the cursor away, and B again goes back inside. SELECT opens a small menu (GO INSIDE or JOURNAL), and **R** starts a firefly round. The clock keeps running out here, so dawn sends you back inside.

Each new star-seed comes with **3 moonflower seeds**.

| Night | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plant a seed (A), then water it (A again). |
| 2 | A sprout. Water it. |
| 3 | A bud, already showing its colour. Water it. |
| 4 | It blooms, and glows at night. |
| 5 to 7 | Still in bloom. |
| The morning after | It fades and drops a new seed (you can hold up to 9). |

A night without water doesn't harm it; it just waits a night longer. Blooms are just for looking at, by night and by day in the yard. The garden starts fresh with each new star-seed, but the journal keeps everything you've found.

**Colours.** A moonflower takes its colour from the care your critter is getting. The more of one kind of care it gets, the likelier that colour is.

| Colour | Grows for a critter that is... |
|---|---|
| SILVER | well cared for all round |
| ROSE | played with a lot |
| GOLD | outside a lot |
| SKY | kept nice and clean |
| MINT | well fed |

**Rare forms.** Now and then a bloom opens as something rarer: **Starbloom**, **Moon Lily**, **Twinglow** or **Aurora**. A rare form is only decided when it blooms, so it's always a surprise. Each one has a riddle in the journal's GARDEN tab. The Twinglow grows two blooms on one stem, in two colours that are different every time.

**Night visitors.** While at least one moonflower glows, visitors come to see them: one to three each night, more when more are in bloom. There are eight regulars (moth, firefly, frog, hedgehog, bat, owl, snail and cricket) and two rare guests (the luna moth and the silver fox). The fox is shy, and only comes when two or more flowers glow.

## Fireflies

On any night in the yard, press **R** to catch fireflies. A round lasts 20 seconds, and you get **three rounds a night**. Steer the jar with the +Control Pad and press **A** to scoop a firefly while it glows, even faintly; an unlit one slips away. B stops a round early.

There are **twelve kinds** to find, each with its own colour and way of blinking. Catching gives no coins or stats: it's all about the collection.

- **Common (3):** out every night.
- **Uncommon (5):** two of them are out each night, a different pair most nights. They glow for shorter.
- **Rare (4):** each needs something special that night. Which ones are out is decided at nightfall, so playing more rounds won't bring them.

A new kind shows its card and goes into the journal's **FIREFLIES** tab, a field guide with hints for the rare ones. The night's catch glows in a lantern on the windowsill until morning, and when your critter wakes the fireflies drift out of the window and fly home. Find all twelve and they come to dance outside the window every night after.

## Outside and coins

From the child stage on, choose **OUTSIDE** from the SELECT menu while your critter is awake, healthy and the lights are on.

- Fresh air makes it happier every 45 seconds or so (30 with a kite out).
- Yard toys and yard decor give it things to play with.
- Coins turn up in the grass, and your critter picks them up for **5 coins** each: up to 3 a day (4 with a pinwheel out).
- It comes back inside by itself when it gets sleepy or sick. Choose GO INSIDE to bring it in sooner.

| Ways to earn coins | Coins |
|---|---|
| Minigames | Up to 15 a round (10 for Left or Right?) |
| Coins in the yard | 5 each, up to 3 or 4 a day |
| Wishing stars | 5 per wish |
| A wonderful farewell | +35 for the next critter |
| A happy farewell | +20 for the next critter |

You can hold up to 9,999 coins.

## The shop and the bag

**The shop** (SELECT) has five things for sale each day: two foods, one more food or care item, and two toys or decor pieces you don't own yet. New toys and decor open up in six steps as your critter grows. Each critter opens 27 of the 33 toys and decor pieces; the other six never come for that critter. Prices are shown on each card.

**The bag** (SELECT) holds everything you buy:

- **Food and care items:** use them straight from the bag.
- **Yard toys:** take one out to the yard at a time.
- **Decor:** place it in a spot. The room has four spots (two on the floor, one on the wall, one on the ceiling) and the yard has two (by the tree and by the house).

Now and then, as your critter wanders past a piece of decor, it uses it and you see what it's doing ("DANCES TO THE MUSIC!"). Using decor doesn't make it happier; it helps the piece's kind of care instead: play pieces count as play, outside pieces as time with plants, rest pieces heal it a little, clean pieces keep a mess from making it sick so soon, and the pond gives hunger +1. A few special pieces give bond, and some are just to look at (DECOR). The journal's ITEMS tab gets a page for each item the first time you buy it, and keeps it forever.

## Farewells

After about 8 years as a grown-up, your critter rises to become a star, around noon. The day before, it gazes up at the stars. How brightly it shines depends on its care score as a grown-up:

| Care score | Ending | Bonus for the next critter |
|---|---|---|
| 80% or more | A wonderful farewell: a bright star | +35 coins |
| 60–79% | A happy farewell | +20 coins |
| Under 60% | A sad goodbye: a dim star | None |

A critter whose health runs out runs away instead, and leaves no star. After any ending a new star-seed falls. Coins start again at 20 plus any bonus, and the bag, toys, decor, shop and garden start fresh. The journal keeps everything.

## The journal

The journal keeps a record of every critter and is never cleared. Open it from the title screen or the SELECT menu (out in the night yard too), and turn its tabs with L and R. When the tabs don't all fit, the strip scrolls, with arrows at its ends:

- **SKY:** a star for every critter you've raised, in its own colour. Pick one to read its story: its name, form, age and what it loved.
- **GUIDE:** a page for each of the 20 forms. A met form shows its picture, lore and whether you've raised it as a boy or a girl. Press A to see how it grows. Secret forms show a riddle until you find them.
- **ITEMS:** a page for each of the 44 shop items, opened the first time you buy one: its picture, price, kind of care and exactly what it does.
- **GARDEN:** every moonflower and night visitor you've found. Press A on a card to look closer. Rare moonflowers show a riddle until you grow them.
- **FIREFLIES:** the field guide to the twelve fireflies. Pick one with the +Control Pad to see its name and what it does, or a hint for one you haven't caught. It also shows how many you've caught in all and your best night.
- **RECORDS:** critters raised, wonderful farewells in a row, forms, secrets and items found, wonderful farewells, games played, wishes made, its most-loved care and coins earned.

## Questions and answers

**My critter ran away. Why?** Its health ran out. Health only drops while it is sick or its hunger is empty, so cure it and feed it quickly when the ! lights up.

**Why can't I go outside?** It has to be at least a child, awake, healthy, and with the lights on. At night while it sleeps, the choice becomes GARDEN instead.

**It's overweight. What do I do?** Go easy on cake, candy and pizza. Salad lowers its weight, and every round of Hop Hop trims it a little.

**I gave it medicine and it made a face.** It wasn't sick. Medicine only helps a sick critter.

**The shop doesn't have the item I want.** The stock changes every day, and new toys and decor open up as your critter grows. A few pieces never come for a given critter; the next one will open a different set.

**What carries over to the next critter?** The journal, with its stars, guide, items and garden pages, and any farewell bonus. Coins, the bag, toys, decor, the shop and the garden start fresh.

**How long does a critter live?** About 12 days in all: half a day as a baby, a day as a child, two as a teen and about 8 as a grown-up.

**Does time pass when the game is off?** No. Your critter waits for you.

**My moonflower didn't grow.** It needs water every night. A missed night doesn't hurt it; it just takes one more night to bloom.

**Why does the moon look different?** It goes through its phases over eight nights. Some rare moonflowers care about that.

**How do I find the rare visitors?** Keep moonflowers blooming. The luna moth visits now and then; the silver fox only when two or more flowers glow.

**What does FAST do?** Everything runs 2.5 times as fast: days, needs, growing up and the garden. Switch it off in the START menu whenever you like.

**Where did JOURNAL go in the START menu?** It moved to the SELECT menu, so you can reach it from the room and the night yard.

**Why didn't a shooting star come tonight?** They're rare now: about one night in three, and just one that night.

**I can't find the last fireflies.** Check the FIREFLIES tab: the rare ones each have a hint. Uncommon ones take turns, so try again on other nights.

**Why doesn't my decor make it happy any more?** Decor helps its kind of care instead: play, plant time, rest, cleanliness, food or bond. Happiness comes from snacks and treats, gifts, yard toys, fresh air, minigames and a bed to wake up in.
